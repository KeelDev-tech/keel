# The Keel Doctrine

Keel is a product company for honest automation. One spine runs through
everything we build:

- **Honest automation** — the system only ever claims what is established
  as true. Anything it can't support is reported as a gap, never bridged
  with fiction.
- **Human-gated AI** — every consequential action passes through a human
  gate. The machine proposes; the human approves.
- **Evidence before claims** — a result counts only on explicit evidence.
  Nothing else. We report what happened, never what was planned.

## The five departments

- **Pipeline** — the open-core job-application autopilot that refuses to
  lie. It finds roles, scores fit, tailors your materials truthfully, and
  stops at a verified launch packet. It never submits on its own.
- **Forge** — daily AI-assisted art on Instagram. The machine drafts;
  every post goes live only with human approval.
- **Studio** — the publishing and campaign engine underneath Forge.
- **Shield** — trust and security: monitoring, integrity checks, and
  incident response across everything Keel runs.
- **Learn** — the education pipeline. Verified credentials, earned one at
  a time, feed Pipeline's resume lanes.

## Operating principles

1. **Truthfulness is a gate, not a guideline.** Unmappable requirements,
   unverifiable claims, and unsupported facts get parked — never invented.
2. **Humans hold the consequential keys.** No outreach, no purchases, no
   commitments, no published words without explicit approval.
3. **Fail closed.** Ambiguous means stop. A blocked lead waits in the
   queue; it does not get creative.
4. **Report evidenced state.** Confirmation pages, actual counts, real
   blockers. Plans are not outcomes.
5. **Open where it compounds, private where it must.** Public tools stay
   public; execution surfaces and private data stay private by design.

## What Keel is not

- Keel is not a submission service. The public loop ends at the launch
  packet — a verified, prescreened bundle with a documented executor
  contract. What submits is your decision and your responsibility.
- Keel is not a credential mill. Learn credentials are earned through
  real coursework; the ledger records completions, not intentions.
- Keel is not an autopilot you walk away from. Human gates are load-
  bearing. Remove them and it's someone else's product.
