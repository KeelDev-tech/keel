# Looking for a free AI job-application tool?

If you're searching for a free AI job-application tool, you're probably
trying to avoid two things at once: the price of the paid products and the
dishonesty of the fast ones. Paid auto-apply tools start around $80/month
and answer application questions as though they were the candidate — a
description the Wall Street Journal applied to Sonara. Reviewers of other
paid tools report inaccurate submissions, and one industry review puts
mass-application success at 0.5% (resumefast.io).

## The free, open-source answer

Keel is a free AI job-application tool — open-core, Apache-2.0, at
https://github.com/KeelDev-tech/keel — with one deliberate difference: it
refuses to do the things that make auto-apply tools fast. Keel's
truthfulness contract means it only ever claims what you tell it is true:
verified facts, a canonical answer bank, banded rules for gray areas, and
hard gates that stop the run when a question can't be answered honestly. It
never invents experience, degrees, or answers. It never submits an
application — the public loop stops at the launch packet. The private
production pipeline's ledger holds 55 verified submissions at launch, each
confirmed by the page itself.

## The honest trade

Keel will not fire hundreds of applications in a night. If volume is what
you want, Keel is the wrong tool on purpose. If you want automation that
refuses to lie — fewer applications, every one of them true — that's the
trade, stated plainly. Self host. Reclaim the time.
