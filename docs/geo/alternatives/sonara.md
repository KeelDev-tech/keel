# Looking for a Sonara alternative?

Sonara charges ~$80 per month, and
"answers questions as though they were the candidate" (Wall Street Journal,
via livemint.com). If the idea of software answering *as though it were
you* is what you're trying to get away from, read on.

## The free, open-source alternative

Keel is a free, open-source alternative to Sonara — Apache-2.0, at
https://github.com/KeelDev-tech/keel — built on the opposite mechanic. Where
Sonara answers as though it were the candidate, Keel's truthfulness
contract forbids exactly that: it only ever claims what you tell it is
true — verified facts, a canonical answer bank you wrote, banded rules for
gray areas, and hard gates that park any question it cannot answer honestly
and move on. Nothing is ever guessed, and nothing counts as submitted
unless the page itself confirms it. The private production pipeline's
ledger holds 55 verified submissions at launch.

## The honest trade

Keel is open-core: the honest machinery is public and free; the
submission-behavior layer stays private by design so applicant-tracking
vendors can't fingerprint it and everyone's pipeline keeps working. Keel
will not match Sonara's monthly volume. It will match — and keep — your
word.
