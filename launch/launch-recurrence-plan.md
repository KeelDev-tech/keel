# Launch recurrence plan — DRAFT (from oss-growth-playbook.md item #9)

Supabase/ToolJet/PostHog pattern: one launch is a spike; a cadence is a flywheel.
Plan #2 and #3 BEFORE #1 so momentum compounds instead of decaying.

## Launch #2 — "Keel Launch Week" (4–6 weeks after #1)

One honest-automation story per day, Supabase-style. Each day is a small,
real, shippable thing — never vapor:

- Day 1: ATS radar expansion — N new boards covered, contributor-credited
- Day 2: Outcome analytics — dashboard panel showing gate-blocked breakdowns
- Day 3: Answer-bank ergonomics — the unblock-forever story (every banked answer unblocks every future lead)
- Day 4: Contributor showcase — name every external contributor, merge their PRs live
- Day 5: The 209→? recount — re-run the ledger recount live, publish the new figure with the same evidence discipline

Channels: dev.to series (one post/day), HN "Launch" on day 1 only, Reddit niche subs for the ATS radar day.

## Launch #3 — "Keel 0.3.0" (8–12 weeks after #1)

The versioned milestone moment: whatever the roadmap delivered, packaged as
a release with a changelog, a fresh demo GIF, and a Show HN "Keel 0.3.0"
second wave (ToolJet 2.0 via r/selfhosted pattern).

## Rules carried forward

- Never launch what isn't usable by strangers without hand-holding (PostHog gate).
- No claims beyond evidence parity — every figure recount-backed.
- License never oscillates: Apache-2.0 public half, settled before #1.
- Each launch names contributors publicly (ToolJet pattern).
